# Silent Mirror

An AI that interacts normally -- but quietly builds a
reflective understanding of the user over time, returning
that understanding as insights that improve self-awareness
and decision-making.

## The idea

Imagine waking up in the morning and asking an AI what
to focus on. It answers helpfully. But quietly, in the
background, it begins to notice patterns. After a week
it says:

I may be wrong -- but it seems like you tend to explore
ideas deeply, but sometimes struggle to act on them.
Does this feel accurate?

## How it works

- Chat-first interface. No dashboards, no forced onboarding.
- Silent observation layer. Tracks patterns across sessions.
- Mode detection. Study, health, general, My Mirror, custom.
- Mirror moments. Tentative insights at natural pauses.
- Growth record. Saved summaries build over time.

## Design philosophy

Machines assist. Humans decide.
Silent Mirror is not an AI you follow.
